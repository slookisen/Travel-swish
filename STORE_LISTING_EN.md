# Travel Swipe — Microsoft Store listing

## Short description

Swipe your way to travel ideas that genuinely match your taste.

## Description

Travel Swipe turns quick, playful choices into a personal travel taste profile—then uses it to rank experiences, restaurants, hotels and organized tours for your destination.

Start with a short trip brief, swipe through simple preference cards, and get a focused list with clear reasons for every match. Save ideas, share results, open official websites or maps, and refine the profile whenever your plans change.

Highlights:

- Fast, adaptive swipe profiling
- Personalized experiences, food, hotels and organized tours
- Clear “why this matches” explanations
- Official website and map links when available
- Saved recommendations and native sharing
- Norwegian and English
- Installable on Windows, iPhone and Android

No account is required. Card answers stay on your device. A calculated preference profile and trip context are sent only when you request live recommendations. You can delete local and related backend data from the taste profile.

Travel Swipe is a discovery and planning tool. Always verify current prices, availability, opening hours and provider terms before you travel or book.
