Privacy by design

Your taste should help your trip—not follow you around.

This policy explains what Travel Swipe stores on your device, what is sent to the recommendation service, and how you can delete it.

Effective date: 1 September 2026 · App: Travel Swipe

Summary

Travel Swipe does not require an account, your real name, an email address or access to social media. Most profile information stays in the app on your device. When you ask for recommendations, the app sends a calculated taste profile and the current trip request to the Travel Swipe service.

You can delete the local profile and the related backend records from Taste profile → Delete my data. The app must be online to confirm deletion from the service.

Information kept on your device

  • answers to swipe cards and manual profile adjustments;
  • destination, trip brief and preferred language;
  • saved recommendations and feedback choices;
  • a randomly generated user identifier and a temporary session identifier.

This information remains until you delete the app data, clear browser storage or use the in-app deletion control. Travel Swipe does not read contacts, photos, precise device location or social media accounts.

Information sent when you use live recommendations

  • the random user and session identifiers;
  • the calculated preference weights—not the complete card-answer history;
  • destination, trip context, selected search type and any optional search text;
  • recommendation run identifiers and voluntary result feedback;
  • standard technical request data such as IP address and user agent, which hosting providers may process for delivery, security and abuse prevention.

The service stores pseudonymous sessions, calculated preferences, recommendation runs and voluntary feedback so it can provide results, prepare the next result set and improve relevance. We do not sell this information or use it for third-party advertising.

Service providers and external links

The web app is delivered through GitHub Pages and the recommendation API is hosted on Render. Destination and search terms may be sent to Google Places and/or Brave Search to retrieve current place information. Those providers receive the search request, not your complete swipe history. Result cards may open official websites, map services or other third-party pages; their own privacy policies then apply.

Sharing

The share button opens your device's share sheet or copies text only after you choose to share. Travel Swipe does not post to social networks automatically. A shared result may contain the place name, destination, match explanation and a link to Travel Swipe.

Deletion and your choices

Open the taste profile and select Delete my data. Travel Swipe asks for confirmation, deletes records linked to the random user identifier from its service, removes local app data and creates a fresh identity the next time it starts. If the service cannot be reached, the app keeps the identifier so you can retry and complete the deletion.

For privacy questions or a deletion problem, use the developer contact shown in the Microsoft Store listing. Do not post your random user identifier in a public issue.

Children

Travel Swipe is a general travel-planning tool and is not designed to collect information from children. It does not knowingly request a child's name, contact details or precise location.

Changes

We may update this policy as the beta service develops. Material changes will be reflected on this page with a new effective date.

Kort norsk sammendrag

Travel Swipe krever ingen konto. Kortsvar og lagrede tips ligger hovedsakelig på enheten. Når du ber om anbefalinger, sendes en beregnet smaksprofil, reisekontekst og en tilfeldig bruker-ID til tjenesten. Du kan slette både lokale data og tilknyttede backend-data fra Smaksprofil → Slett mine data.
